ReelForge-es

ReelForge-es is a small, self-hosted editorial and analytics tool used by one creator to manage the short videos of the education channel Por la Tangente (@porlatangente.es): math and physics explained in 20-second animations, in Spanish.

Connect your account

The app runs on the owner's own computer. The button below opens the local dashboard at http://localhost:8080 and starts the standard TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0) authorization. You will be asked to approve the permissions listed below on TikTok's own page.

Connect with TikTok

If nothing opens, the local dashboard is not running on this computer. After you approve, TikTok redirects to callback.html, which hands the temporary authorization code to the local dashboard.

What the dashboard does

  • Profile and stats — shows the connected account's profile and public counts.
  • Video list — lists the account's own videos with their public metrics, to compare what works.
  • Posting — the owner selects a rendered MP4, writes the title, picks a privacy level (only the options TikTok allows for that account) and sends it with the Content Posting API. Nothing is posted without that explicit action.

Permissions requested (TikTok scopes)

ScopeUsed for
user.info.basicDisplay name and avatar of the connected account.
user.info.statsFollower, like and video counts shown in the dashboard.
video.listList the account's own public videos and their view/like/comment/share counts.
video.uploadSend a video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
video.publishPublish a video the creator has just reviewed and approved.
