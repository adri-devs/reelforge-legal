Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what ReelForge-es ("the Tool") does with data obtained when you connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.

1. Data we access

DataScopePurpose
Display name, avatar, open IDuser.info.basicShow which account is connected.
Follower, following, like and video countsuser.info.statsShow account statistics in the dashboard.
The account's own videos and their public countsvideo.listCompare the performance of the creator's videos.
Videos the creator chooses to sendvideo.upload, video.publishPost or draft a video the creator approved.

We do not access private messages, contacts, or any other user's data, and we do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

2. OAuth tokens

Access and refresh tokens are used exclusively to publish the creator's own videos and read their own metrics. They are stored only in a local, access-restricted configuration file on the Operator's computer (never in a public repository, never on this website), and are renewed or deleted when you revoke access.

3. Storage, sharing and retention

  • Data stays on the Operator's computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • Metrics are kept only as long as they are useful for the creator's own analysis; tokens are deleted on revocation or on request.
  • This website (GitHub Pages) does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and receives no TikTok data: the callback page only forwards a temporary authorization code to localhost inside your own browser.

4. Your rights and revocation

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services), and ask for deletion of any stored data by opening an issue at github.com/adri-devs/reelforge-legal. Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification and erasure.

5. Children

The Tool is not directed to children under 16 and does not knowingly process their data.

6. Changes

We will update this page and its date if the processing changes.
